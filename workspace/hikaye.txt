Once upon a time, in a quiet village nestled between hills and forests, lived a curious child named Ali. Every day, he explored the woods, fascinated by the mysteries around him. One twilight, while chasing a firefly, Ali stumbled upon a hidden glade. In the center stood an ancient oak tree, its roots forming a circle. Beneath it, he found a small, glowing key. Intrigued, Ali followed the key’s light deeper into the forest, where he discovered a moss-covered door leading to a hidden library. The air was thick with the scent of old books, and shelves stretched endlessly. Each book whispered tales of bravery, love, and adventure. Ali spent days reading, learning from each story. When he finally returned home, he shared the tales with his friends, sparking a love for stories in the village. From then on, the village held annual storytelling festivals, and Ali became a beloved storyteller. The end.
